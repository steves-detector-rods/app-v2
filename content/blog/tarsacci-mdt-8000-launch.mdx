As of this week we're shipping two-piece carbon-fiber lowers for the Tarsacci MDT 8000.
We've had this on the roadmap for nearly a year and spent the last eight months in
back-and-forth with Tarsacci's engineering team to make sure the geometry matches
theirs exactly.

## Why this took so long

The MDT 8000 uses a proprietary lower-rod geometry that doesn't match any of the
Minelab, Garrett, or XP machines we already support. We needed:

- A spec sheet from Tarsacci (which required becoming an authorized dealer first)
- Several prototype builds to validate clevis fit
- A production run with our carbon-fiber supplier at a non-standard tube diameter
- Stress-testing in wet conditions (the MDT is a known waterproof machine)

## What you get

- 24" extended length, collapses to 15" for travel
- Locking stainless ferrule (same design we use on the Equinox 2-piece)
- All-stainless hardware
- Matches factory geometry, drops right in without shims or modification

Available now at $89.
