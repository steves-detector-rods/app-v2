## What we collect

When you place an order we collect the information needed to fulfill it: your name,
shipping and billing address, email, and (for custom builds) any specs you send us.
We don't store payment details — those go directly to Stripe and PayPal and we never
see them.

When you contact us via the contact form we collect your name, email, and message so
we can reply.

When you sign up for the newsletter we collect your email so we can send you
occasional updates (new products, how-tos, sales). You can unsubscribe from every
email we send.

## What we use it for

- **Order data** — used to build and ship your order. We keep order records for tax
  and warranty purposes.
- **Newsletter email** — used only for our newsletter. We don't share the list.
- **Analytics** (Google Analytics 4 + Vercel Analytics) — anonymized page-view data
  for understanding traffic patterns. No personal identification.

## What we don't do

We don't sell your information. We don't rent it. We don't share it with marketing
networks.

## Payment processing

Card and PayPal payments are handled by Stripe, PayPal, and Snipcart (our checkout
vendor). Their privacy policies govern how they handle payment data:

- Stripe: https://stripe.com/privacy
- PayPal: https://www.paypal.com/privacy
- Snipcart: https://snipcart.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies for the cart state (so your cart survives a page
refresh) and analytics. Snipcart uses its own cookies for cart and checkout.

## Your rights

Email steve@stevesdetectorrods.com to:

- Request a copy of the data we have on you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and order history (note: we may need to retain some data for
  tax purposes)
- Unsubscribe from marketing emails
