## Returns

We accept returns within **30 days of delivery** on unused standard items for a full
refund (less original shipping). The item needs to come back in new condition — no
scratches, no install marks, no cam-lock indentations.

### What's non-returnable

Custom builds are made to your exact spec and can't be resold, so they're
non-returnable unless there's a manufacturing defect:

- Tall Man / custom-length lower rods
- Custom colors
- Counterweight-ready configurations
- 3-piece upgrades
- Any scoop handle you specified by brand

If you think a custom item has a defect, email Steve with photos — we'll make it
right.

### How to return

Email steve@stevesdetectorrods.com with your order number. We'll send you an RMA
number and a return address. Pack carefully — carbon-fiber tubes survive almost
anything except being crushed in the mail.

## Warranty

### Lifetime on the carbon

Our carbon-fiber tubes carry a **lifetime warranty** against manufacturing defects:
delamination, catastrophic cracking, tube separation from fittings. If a tube fails
under normal use, we'll replace it at no cost.

### One year on hardware

Cam locks, end caps, hinges, ferrules, and stainless fasteners carry a **one-year
warranty**. Most hardware lasts 5+ years in normal use; this covers early-life
failures.

### What's not covered

- Damage from dropping the detector on concrete
- Damage from crushing the shaft in a car door (yes, this happens)
- Corrosion from cleaners not rated for carbon fiber
- Normal wear on grip materials
- Modifications (drilling extra holes, filing the clevis, etc.)

## Custom-build satisfaction

Custom builds aren't returnable, but if you're unhappy with one, email Steve. We've
re-cut Tall Man rods that came out slightly long, rebuilt cuffs that didn't sit right,
and swapped colors when a customer's partner vetoed their choice. We can't promise
a redo every time, but we care about the people we build for.
